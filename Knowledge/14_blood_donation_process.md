# The Blood Donation Process — Step by Step

## Overview

The end-to-end blood donation process takes about **45 minutes to one hour**. The actual blood collection takes only 8–10 minutes for whole blood.

## Step 1: Registration

- Present a valid photo ID (driver's license, passport, or donor card)
- Read basic educational materials about donation risks
- Review medication and travel restriction lists

## Step 2: Health History Questionnaire

- Complete a private, confidential questionnaire
- Covers sexual history, recent travel, medical conditions, and medication use
- Ensures the safety of the blood supply

## Step 3: Mini-Physical

A health professional checks your vital signs:

- **Blood pressure**
- **Pulse**
- **Body temperature** (must not have a fever)
- **Hemoglobin level** (checked via a quick finger prick)

## Step 4: The Donation

- Sit or lie in a comfortable reclining chair
- Phlebotomist cleans your arm and inserts a sterile needle
- Approximately 450–500 mL (one pint) of blood is collected
- The actual draw takes **8–10 minutes**

## Step 5: Refreshments and Recovery

- Rest in an observation area for **10–15 minutes**
- Drink fluids and eat a light snack
- Leave only when you feel fully recovered

## Apheresis Donations

Platelet or plasma donations via apheresis take longer — typically **1 to 2 hours** — and involve a machine that separates and returns components.
