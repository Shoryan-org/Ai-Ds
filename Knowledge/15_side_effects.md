# Side Effects of Blood Donation

## Overview

Blood donation is highly regulated and very safe for healthy adults. However, a small percentage of donors may experience mild side effects.

## Common Mild Side Effects

**Dizziness or Lightheadedness**
Some donors feel faint due to a slight drop in blood pressure. If this happens, lie down and elevate your legs.

**Bruising**
A small bruise may form at the needle site if blood leaks into surrounding tissue.

**Soreness**
The arm used for donation may feel slightly stiff or sore for a day.

**Fatigue**
You may feel slightly tired on the day of your donation.

## Prevention Tips

- Eat a substantial meal before donating
- Hydrate well before and after the procedure
- Avoid strenuous exercise and heavy lifting for 24 hours

## When to Seek Help

Contact the blood donor center or a healthcare provider if you experience:

- Bleeding from the needle site that does not stop with pressure
- Severe dizziness that persists for hours
- Pain, numbness, or tingling running down your arm into your fingers

## Can You Catch a Disease from Donating?

**No.** You cannot catch HIV or any other infectious disease from donating blood. Blood centers use single-use, sterile needles and equipment for every donor, which are safely discarded immediately after use.
