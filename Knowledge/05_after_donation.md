# After Donation — Recovery and Care

## Immediate Care

**Rest**
Remain seated in the recovery area for **10–15 minutes** after donation.

**Refreshments**
Have a light snack and drink fluids (water, juice) provided by the clinic. This restores blood sugar and hydration levels.

## First 24 Hours

**Hydration**
Drink plenty of extra water or non-alcoholic fluids over the next 24 hours.

**Physical Activity**
Avoid heavy lifting, intense exercise, and strenuous physical exertion for the rest of the day. This helps prevent dizziness and bruising.

**Bandage Care**
- Keep the bandage clean and dry
- Leave it on for at least 4–5 hours
- If bleeding resumes, raise your arm straight up and apply firm pressure for 5–10 minutes

## How Your Body Recovers

- **Plasma** (fluid volume) is replaced within 24–48 hours
- **Red blood cells** regenerate within 4–6 weeks

## When to Seek Help

Contact the blood donor center or a healthcare provider if you experience:

- Bleeding from the needle site that does not stop with pressure
- Severe dizziness that persists for hours
- Pain, numbness, or tingling running down your arm into your fingers
