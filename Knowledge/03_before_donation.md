# Before Donation — How to Prepare

## Hydration

Drink extra water or non-alcoholic fluids before your appointment. Being well-hydrated:

- Makes veins easier to find
- Helps prevent dizziness during and after donation
- Reduces the risk of feeling faint

Aim for about **500 mL (16 ounces)** of extra fluid before donating.

## Diet

**Eat a healthy meal**
Have a balanced meal within a few hours of your appointment. **Do not donate on an empty stomach.**

**Avoid fatty foods**
Foods high in fat can interfere with mandatory blood testing. Choose lean proteins, whole grains, and vegetables.

**Boost iron intake**
In the weeks before donating, eat iron-rich foods to maintain healthy hemoglobin levels:

- Lean red meat
- Beans and lentils
- Spinach and leafy greens
- Fortified cereals
- Tofu

## Sleep

Get **7–8 hours of sleep** the night before your donation. Being well-rested helps your body recover more easily.

## What to Bring

- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport, or donor card)
- List of current medications (prescription and over-the-counter)
- Travel history — be ready to share destinations and dates from the past few years

## Clothing

Wear comfortable clothes with sleeves that can be easily rolled up above the elbow.

## Medication Check

Continue taking prescribed medications. Most common medications do **not** prevent donation. Never stop a prescribed medication just to donate.

## If You Feel Unwell

Cancel and reschedule if you have:

- Fever
- Cold or flu symptoms
- Sore throat
- Stomach upset
